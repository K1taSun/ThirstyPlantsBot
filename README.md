# ThirstyPlantsBot

A free, open-source Telegram bot where every houseplant has its own profile.

Plants remind you to water them, chat with you and your roommates, and help figure out why their leaves are drooping.

## Features (planned)
-  Watering reminders with Done / Snooze / Not today buttons
-  Plant profiles with a care "passport"
-  Species recognition from a photo
-  Shared homes with roommates
-  Talk to your plants

## Stack
Python · aiogram 3 · PostgreSQL/SQLite · APScheduler

## Status
Early planning.
